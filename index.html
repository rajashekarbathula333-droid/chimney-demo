Before your visit

Simple tips for a smooth service

Do

  • Clear the space under the chimney
  • Tell us the brand and model if you know it
  • Mention any noise, smell or drip you noticed

Don't

  • Don't open or force the motor or blower yourself
  • Don't use harsh chemicals on the filters
  • Don't wait for suction to drop completely
